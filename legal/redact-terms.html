3. What Redact Can and Can't Do

Redact is built to remove hidden metadata, like GPS location, device details, and timestamps, from photos and videos, and to help you see what's in a file before you share it. We work hard to make it thorough, but no tool can promise to find and remove every piece of identifying information in every file. In particular, keep in mind that

  • file formats keep changing, and some cameras and apps store data in unusual or undocumented places that Redact may not recognize,
  • removing metadata doesn't change what's visible in the picture or video itself, like faces, street signs, landmarks, screens, documents, reflections, or sounds in a video's audio track,
  • a file's name can reveal information on its own, and the apps or services you share a file through may add their own data,
  • areas you cover with Censor are only as private as the coverage you choose, so check the result before sharing it,
  • converting a file can change its quality, size, or compatibility.

Before you share anything sensitive, check the cleaned file with Scan, look at the content yourself, and keep a backup of any original you want to keep. You are responsible for deciding what you share and with whom. If your safety depends on a file being completely anonymous, don't rely on Redact, or any single tool, alone.
