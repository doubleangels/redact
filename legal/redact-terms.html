1. Overview

Redact is a free, open-source Android app that strips hidden metadata, things like GPS location, device details, timestamps, and camera settings, out of your photos and videos. It also lets you look inside a file before you share it, convert between formats, and cover up parts of a picture, all without anything ever leaving your phone.

These Terms of Use explain the ground rules for using it: what Redact does, what it honestly can't promise, what we ask of you, and where our responsibility ends. We've written them in the same plain language as our Privacy Policy, because legal pages shouldn't need a lawyer to understand.

Here's the short version: Redact exists because we care deeply about privacy and believe nobody should be tracked just for using an app. We give it away for free, we try hard to make it thorough, and we're upfront about its limits. In return, we ask you to use it lawfully, check your files before sharing anything sensitive, and understand that it comes without guarantees.

5. What Redact Does

Every one of Redact's features runs entirely on your phone. Here's what each one is for.

FeatureWhat It DoesWhere It Runs
Clean Removes hidden metadata like GPS location, device details, and timestamps, and saves a cleaned copy. On Your Phone
Scan Shows you the metadata inside a photo or video, so you can see what it would reveal. On Your Phone
Convert Converts photos and videos between formats, without carrying the metadata across. On Your Phone
Censor Lets you cover parts of a photo, like faces or license plates, before you share it. On Your Phone
Share to Redact Cleans, scans, converts, or censors files you share into Redact from other apps. On Your Phone
Quick Settings tiles and shortcuts Open Clean, Scan, or Censor straight from your phone's quick settings or home screen. On Your Phone
Privacy Stats Counts how many files you've cleaned and what was removed, as plain numbers. On Your Phone

Redact needs Android 12 or newer. We test it carefully, but we can't promise it works on every device, Android version, or custom system, now or in the future.

6. What Redact Can't Guarantee

We work hard to make Redact thorough, and we test it against a wide range of cameras and file formats. But we want to be completely honest with you: no tool can promise to find and remove every piece of identifying information in every file, and Redact is no exception.

What to Keep in MindWhy It Matters
Unusual or new file formats Cameras and apps keep inventing new places to store data, and some of those places are undocumented. Redact may not recognize all of them.
What's visible in the picture Removing metadata doesn't change what the photo or video shows. Faces, street signs, landmarks, screens, documents, reflections, and the sound in a video can all reveal where you are or who you are.
File names A file's name can give information away on its own, like a date or a place.
The apps you share through Messaging apps, social networks, and cloud services may add their own data, or keep their own copies, after a file leaves Redact.
Censored areas Censor only covers what you choose to cover. Check the result before sharing it.
Converted files Converting can change a file's quality, size, colors, or compatibility with other apps.
Deleting originals If you turn on Delete Originals After Cleaning, Android moves the originals to the trash once you confirm. Anything later removed from the trash is gone for good.
Before you share something sensitive: open the cleaned file in Scan to check what's left, look at the picture or video itself with fresh eyes, and keep a backup of any original you want to keep. If your safety depends on a file being completely anonymous, please don't rely on Redact, or any single tool, alone.
