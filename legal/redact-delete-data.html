How to Ask Us to Delete Your Data

Redact is made by DoubleAngels, an independent developer, and this page is how you ask us to delete data about you from the Redact app on Google Play. It all happens over email, and it only applies if you've emailed us for support before.

Send your request to jzgm2llm@addy.io
  1. Write to us from the same address you used before. That's how we find your emails, and how we know the request is really yours, since Redact has no accounts to log in to.
  2. Use this subject line. Privacy Request: Data Deletion
  3. Tell us what you'd like deleted. You can send something like the message below. If you've written to us from more than one address, list them all.
  4. We delete the emails and let you know. We'll reply to confirm once it's done. Requests are free, and we'll answer within the time the law that applies to you allows, usually 30 days.

If You've Never Emailed Us

Then we don't have anything from you, and there's nothing to delete. Redact doesn't collect your data, so you don't need to send a request. The only emails we keep are the ones people send to jzgm2llm@addy.io asking for help, reporting a problem, or making a privacy request like this one.

What Happens to Your Emails

When you ask, we delete every email to and from your address, including anything you wrote or attached. Deleting them is permanent, and it means we lose the record of our conversation. If you write to us about the same problem later, we'll be starting fresh.

If we can't be sure a request comes from the address owner, we'll ask a couple of questions first, as the Your Rights section of the Privacy Policy explains.

1. What We Can't Delete

Anonymous Crash Reports

Crash reporting is off unless you turn on Send Crash Reports yourself. When it's on, the reports contain nothing that identifies you, no name, email, install ID, or file contents. Because the data is anonymous, we can't find, show, or delete any one person's reports. Nothing in them tells us which reports came from you. Sentry removes them on its own after its retention period for our plan, currently 90 days for crash and error reports and 30 days for performance data and logs. See Sentry's data retention periods.

If you want no more reports sent, switch Send Crash Reports off in Redact's settings, it takes effect immediately. The Optional Crash Reporting section of the Privacy Policy has the full detail.

Google Play Order Records

When you buy Redact, Google handles the payment and shares order details with us. Tax and accounting laws require us to keep sales records, usually for up to 7 years, so we can't delete these on request. Google keeps its own records too, under its own policies. To manage the data Google holds about you, use your Google Account settings.

GitHub

Anything you posted on our GitHub page is public and controlled by GitHub and by you. You can edit or delete your own issues and comments there.

2. Data on Your Phone

Everything Redact works with stays on your phone, so it's yours to manage. We never see any of it.

  • Cleaned and converted files are ordinary files in Pictures/Redact and Movies/Redact, or in the folders you picked. Delete them like any other photo or video.
  • Settings and statistics stay on your phone until you clear Redact's storage in Android's app settings, or uninstall Redact.
  • Temporary copies made while sharing are deleted shortly afterward, and anything left over is cleared after 24 hours when Clear Cache on Startup is on.
