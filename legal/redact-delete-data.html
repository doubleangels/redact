How to Request Deletion of Your Data

This page explains how to ask DoubleAngels, the developer of the Redact app on Google Play, to delete data about you.

  1. Use the form below, or write to jzgm2llm@addy.io. Enter the email address you have used to contact us.
  2. Send the email from that address, with Privacy Request in the subject line. Sending from that address is how we confirm the request is yours, since Redact has no accounts to log in to.
  3. We delete every email to and from that address in our email account, and reply to confirm. Requests are free and answered within the time the law allows, usually 30 days.

If you've never emailed us, we hold no data about you, because Redact doesn't collect any. If we can't be sure a request comes from the address owner, we'll ask a couple of questions first, as the Your Rights section of the Privacy Policy explains.

What Is Deleted and What Is Kept

These are all the types of data about you that could exist, what happens to each when you ask, and how long it is kept if you don't.

Data When You Ask for Deletion How Long It Is Kept Otherwise
Emails you sent us, with your address and anything you wrote or attached Deleted Every email to and from your address. Until you ask us to delete them.
Photos, videos, settings and statistics Never collected We never receive them, so there is nothing to delete. Only on your phone, until you delete them, clear Redact's storage or uninstall the app.
Anonymous crash reports (only if you turn on Send Crash Reports) Cannot be deleted Nothing in them links them to you. Turn the setting off to stop new ones. Up to 90 days at Sentry, then removed automatically.
Order details from Google Play Kept Tax and accounting laws require us to keep sales records. Usually up to 7 years. Google also keeps its own records under its own policies.
GitHub issues and comments Not held by us They are public and controlled by GitHub and by you. You can delete your own posts. Until you or GitHub delete them.

1. What We Can't Delete

Anonymous Crash Reports

Crash reporting is off unless you turn on Send Crash Reports yourself. When it's on, the reports carry no name, email, install ID or file contents, so nothing in them tells us which reports came from you. That means we can't find, show or delete any one person's reports. Sentry keeps them for up to 90 days and then removes them on its own.

To stop more from being sent, switch Send Crash Reports off in Redact's settings. It takes effect right away. The Optional Crash Reporting section of the Privacy Policy has the full detail.

Google Play Order Records

When you buy Redact, Google handles the payment and shares order details with us. Tax and accounting laws require us to keep sales records, usually for up to 7 years, so we can't delete these on request. Google also keeps its own records under its own policies. To manage or delete your Google account data, use your Google Account settings.

GitHub

Anything you posted on our GitHub page is public and controlled by GitHub and by you. You can edit or delete your own issues and comments there.

2. Data on Your Phone

Everything Redact works with stays on your phone, and you're in charge of it.

  • Cleaned and converted files are ordinary files in Pictures/Redact and Movies/Redact, or in the folders you picked. Delete them like any other photo or video.
  • Settings and statistics stay on your phone until you clear Redact's storage in Android's app settings, or uninstall Redact.
  • Temporary copies made while sharing are deleted shortly after, and cleared after 24 hours when Clear Cache on Startup is on.
