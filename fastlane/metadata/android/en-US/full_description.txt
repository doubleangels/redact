<b>Your photos can reveal where you live.</b>

<p>Every photo your phone takes can carry hidden metadata: the exact GPS location where it was taken, the date and time, and the make and model of your phone. Anyone you send the original file to can read it. Redact shows you what a photo or video reveals and removes it, entirely on your device. No account, no uploads, no ads and no data collected.</p>

<b>Who it's for</b>
<ul>
<li>Selling on Marketplace, eBay or Craigslist without giving away your home address</li>
<li>Sending photos to a date, a landlord or someone you just met</li>
<li>Sharing pictures of your kids, your home or your car</li>
<li>Journalists, activists and anyone protecting a source</li>
<li>Emailing photos or sending them as files, which keeps every hidden detail</li>
</ul>

<b>See it: Scan</b>
<p>Open any photo or video and see every hidden field in a clear, searchable list. A Location badge marks anything that shows where it was taken.</p>

<b>Remove it: Clean</b>
<p>Clean up to 100 photos and videos at once. Redact removes GPS, device details, timestamps, XMP, IPTC, comments and video tags, plus data hidden after the picture such as extra preview images and Motion Photo clips, then tells you exactly what it removed. Your originals stay untouched unless you ask Redact to move them to the trash.</p>

<b>Share safely from any app</b>
<p>Share a photo or video to Redact from your gallery or any other app. It cleans the file and hands it straight back to the share sheet. Or share it to Scan with Redact to check it first.</p>

<b>Convert formats</b>
<p>JPEG, PNG, WebP and HEIC images, and H.264, H.265, VP9 and AV1 video. Every converted file is stripped and checked.</p>

<b>Private by design</b>
<ul>
<li>Everything runs on your phone. Cleaning, scanning and converting need no internet connection.</li>
<li>No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account</li>
<li>Google Play Data safety: no data collected, no data shared</li>
<li>Open source under GPL-3.0, so anyone can read the code on GitHub</li>
<li>Works with the system file picker, so access to your whole library is optional</li>
<li>Optional crash reports stay off unless you turn them on</li>
</ul>

<b>Handy extras</b>
<p>A Quick Settings tile, home screen shortcuts, your own output folders, Keep Original File Names, Strict Clean, hiding Redact from screenshots and recents, and automatic clearing of copied metadata. Statistics shows what Redact has removed, counted only on your phone.</p>

<b>Pay once, keep it</b>
<p>No subscription, no ads and no in-app purchases. Your purchase supports an independent developer.</p>
