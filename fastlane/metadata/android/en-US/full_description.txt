<b>Redact: Privacy & Metadata Remover</b>

<p>Protect your privacy with Redact — remove hidden EXIF metadata from photos and videos, inspect what is embedded before you share, and convert formats locally on your device.</p>

<b>Key Features:</b>

<ul>
<li><b>Complete Privacy Protection:</b><br>
Remove GPS location, device details, timestamps, camera settings, and other hidden metadata. Video cleaning uses remuxing or full transcode (your choice in Settings) while preserving playback quality.</li>

<li><b>Metadata Viewer:</b><br>
Scan any photo or video to see organized EXIF and container metadata before you clean it. Copy values or send a file to Clean or Convert.</li>

<li><b>Clean, Scan, Convert &amp; Share:</b><br>
Clean metadata in batches (up to 20 files). Convert image/video formats without uploading to a server — then run Clean if you also want metadata removed from converted files. Share into Redact from any app; cancel long jobs or continue with partial success if one file fails. No hard file-size cap; storage and memory warnings appear before large jobs.</li>

<li><b>Privacy-First Permissions:</b><br>
On Android 13+, browse and pick files from Downloads, Files, or gallery folders with the system document picker—no broad library access required. Optional photo-location permission unlocks GPS fields in Scan. Crash reporting and notifications are off by default.</li>

<li><b>100% Local Processing:</b><br>
Cleaning, scanning, and conversion run on your device. Optional anonymized crash diagnostics (Sentry) never upload your media. Temporary cache can be cleared manually or pruned on startup.</li>

<li><b>Open Source &amp; Ad-Free:</b><br>
No ads or behavioral tracking. Source code is available on GitHub for community review.</li>

<li><b>13 Languages:</b><br>
English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified &amp; Traditional), Hindi, and Arabic.</li>
</ul>

<p>EXIF metadata can reveal GPS coordinates, device model, and capture settings. Redact helps you share on your terms — with quality preserved and your files kept on your phone.</p>

<p><b>How It Works:</b></p>
<p>Select files in Redact or share from another app, tap Clean Metadata, and save privacy-safe copies to your gallery. For share-in, Redact strips metadata before you forward files onward. Strict Clean and share paths always remove location data.</p>
