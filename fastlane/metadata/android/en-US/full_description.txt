<b>Your photos can reveal where you live.</b>

Every photo and video can carry the exact GPS location, the date and time, and your phone's make and model. Anyone you send the original file to can read it.

Redact is a metadata remover and EXIF remover. It shows what a file reveals and strips it, entirely on your device.

No account, no uploads, no ads and no data collected.

<b>Who it's for</b>
<ul>
<li>Selling on Marketplace, eBay or Craigslist without giving away your home address</li>
<li>Sending photos to a date, a landlord or someone you just met</li>
<li>Sharing pictures of your kids, your home or your car</li>
<li>Journalists, activists and anyone protecting a source</li>
<li>Emailing photos or sending them as files, which keeps every hidden detail</li>
</ul>

<b>See what a photo reveals with Scan</b>
<ul>
<li>Every hidden field in a clear, searchable EXIF viewer</li>
<li>A Location badge marks anything that shows where it was taken</li>
</ul>

<b>Remove GPS, location and EXIF data with Clean</b>
<ul>
<li>Clean up to 100 photos and videos at once</li>
<li>Removes GPS and geotags, device details and timestamps</li>
<li>Removes XMP, IPTC, comments and video tags</li>
<li>Removes hidden extras like preview images and Motion Photo clips</li>
<li>Tells you exactly what it removed</li>
<li>Your originals stay untouched unless you ask Redact to trash them</li>
</ul>

<b>Share safely from any app</b>
Share a photo or video to Redact from your gallery or any other app. It cleans the file and hands it straight back to the share sheet.

Or share it to Scan with Redact to check it first.

<b>Convert formats and hide details</b>
<ul>
<li>Convert HEIC to JPG, plus PNG and WebP images</li>
<li>Convert H.264, H.265, VP9 and AV1 video</li>
<li>Every converted file is stripped and checked</li>
<li>Censor pixelates, blacks out or blurs faces, plates and addresses</li>
</ul>
